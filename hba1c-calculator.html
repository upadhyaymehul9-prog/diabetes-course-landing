What the ranges mean

The ADA uses these HbA1c ranges for adults who are not pregnant:3

  • Below 5.7%: below the prediabetes range.
  • 5.7% to 6.4%: the prediabetes range. See borderline diabetes: what to do.
  • 6.5% or higher: the diabetes range.

One abnormal result is usually not enough. Unless there are clear signs of very high sugar, the ADA says diagnosis needs two abnormal results, either from different tests or from the same test at two different times.3 Your doctor makes the diagnosis.

For a plain explanation of each number, read HbA1c numbers explained.

When HbA1c can mislead

Estimates like this are made for most people with type 1 and type 2 diabetes.2 They come from a study of 507 people, including 268 with type 1 diabetes, 159 with type 2 diabetes and 80 without diabetes.2 They are averages, and your own result can differ.

Ask your doctor if any of these apply to you. Conditions that change how long red blood cells live can alter HbA1c. Examples are thalassemia, other hemoglobin variants and iron-deficiency anaemia.4 In these cases HbA1c can read higher or lower than your true average sugar. Your doctor may use fasting or after-meal sugar tests instead.

Also, your meter readings and your lab HbA1c may not match exactly. A lab test is the one used for diagnosis.

What to do with your result

  1. Write it down with the date, and compare it next time. See tracking your numbers.
  2. If you are in the prediabetes range, small changes now work best. Start with borderline diabetes: what to do and the post-meal walk.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula to convert HbA1c to average blood sugar?

Average glucose in mg/dL equals 28.7 times the HbA1c percentage, minus 46.7. For example, an HbA1c of 7% gives about 154 mg/dL. The formula comes from the A1C-Derived Average Glucose (ADAG) study.

What average blood sugar is an HbA1c of 6.5%?

About 140 mg/dL, or 7.8 mmol/L, according to the American Diabetes Association's eAG table. 6.5% is also the lower edge of the diabetes range, but a diagnosis needs confirmation by your doctor.

What is a normal HbA1c?

Below 5.7% is under the prediabetes range. 5.7% to 6.4% is the prediabetes range. 6.5% or higher is the diabetes range, according to the ADA.

Is this calculator accurate for everyone?

It is an estimate based on averages. Conditions that affect red blood cells, such as thalassemia or iron-deficiency anaemia, can make HbA1c misleading. Ask your doctor if this applies to you.

Can I use my meter readings to work out my HbA1c?

Only roughly. A few single readings are not an average over three months. A glucose monitor worn for weeks gives a better average. A lab HbA1c is still the standard test.

Does this calculator store my numbers?

No. The calculation happens on your device in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or saved.
