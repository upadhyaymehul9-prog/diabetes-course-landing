Dr. Mehul Upadhyay has spent 15+ years working inside hospital operations and healthcare quality management — including time in the New Zealand healthcare system. His day job is making sure hospitals follow strict, evidence-based standards, not guesswork. This course applies that same structured thinking to the gap most patients hit after the first few months: the plateau.

The evidence: The UK's DiRECT trial found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes — not medication alone — put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission. Structure, tracked consistently, is what moved the number. Not everyone gets there, but it's worth trying seriously.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Will this replace my medication?

No, and it's not designed to. This course gives you structure and tracking alongside your treatment — never stop or change medication without talking to your doctor first.

I've tried structured plans before and given up. Why is this different?

Most plans fail because there's no tracking system showing you what's actually working. This course builds that in from week one, so plateaus become visible and fixable instead of invisible and discouraging.

How long until I see a difference?

This is a 12-week structured plan. Some weeks will feel great, some will feel frustrating — that's normal and covered directly in the course, not glossed over.
