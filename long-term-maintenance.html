Before you start

Do I have to follow the plan this strictly forever?

No. The aim is a sustainable, lower-effort maintenance routine — not an indefinite continuation of the most intense early weeks.

What if my numbers rise again after I've improved them?

Review it with your healthcare team and your recent habits using the tracking system — don't change medication independently based on a single reading.

Does remission mean I can stop paying attention to diabetes?

No. Even when remission occurs, ongoing monitoring and follow-up with your doctor remain important — this course's maintenance module covers exactly that.
