Before you start

Do I have to follow the plan forever?

The aim is sustainable habits, not a temporary punishment.

What if my numbers rise again?

Review the situation with your healthcare team and your habits; do not change medication independently.

Does remission mean I can ignore diabetes?

No. Even when remission occurs, ongoing follow-up remains important.
