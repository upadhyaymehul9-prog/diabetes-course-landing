Before you ask

Is fasting safe if I'm on insulin or diabetes tablets?

It can be, but only with your doctor's involvement — some medications need dose adjustments before you change your eating pattern. This course tells you what to ask, but never replaces that conversation.

What's the difference between this and just skipping breakfast?

Unstructured meal-skipping often leads to overeating later and unpredictable glucose swings. A structured approach is planned, consistent, and tracked — which is what actually makes it safe and effective.

Will this help me fast safely during Navratri or Ramadan?

The course teaches the underlying principles of safe fasting with diabetes, which apply to religious fasting too — but always check your specific plan with your doctor beforehand.
