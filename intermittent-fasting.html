The honest answer

It depends entirely on your medication. If you're on insulin or a sulfonylurea — medications that lower blood sugar directly — fasting windows can push you into dangerous low blood sugar (hypoglycemia) without food to buffer the medication's effect. This isn't a decision to make on your own; it needs your doctor, who knows exactly which medication and dose you're on.

If you're not on glucose-lowering medication and your doctor clears it, some people do find structured fasting windows helpful. But it is never a default recommendation in this course — the risk profile depends entirely on your individual treatment, and getting it wrong isn't a minor mistake.
