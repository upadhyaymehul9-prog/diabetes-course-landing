A Realistic Progression

From walking upward, at your own pace

Start with short walks at your current ability — no step count target, no comparison to anyone else's routine.

Add a short walk after meals specifically, since timing movement around food changes how it affects your blood sugar.

Gradually increase duration and frequency as the habit holds, well before adding intensity.

Protect your sleep and manage stress alongside movement — both quietly cancel out exercise's benefit when ignored.

Before You Start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. The course does not require a gym membership or special equipment — it's built around walking and bodyweight movement you can do at home.

What if I'm currently quite inactive?

The progression starts from your current ability. Follow appropriate clinical guidance if you have existing mobility or cardiovascular concerns.

Can exercise replace my medication?

No. Never stop or change prescribed medication based on exercise results — always consult your doctor first.
