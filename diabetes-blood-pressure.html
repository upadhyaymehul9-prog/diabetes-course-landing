The Evidence

Shared drivers, not two separate conditions

Type 2 diabetes and hypertension share insulin resistance, excess weight, and chronic stress as common drivers, which is why structured weight loss and stress reduction are recommended approaches for improving both together, rather than treating each in isolation.
