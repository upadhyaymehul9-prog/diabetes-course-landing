Before you ask

Is this a meditation or stress-management course?

No — it's a structured diabetes and weight-loss program where sleep and stress are one module alongside nutrition, movement, and tracking, not the whole course.

Will fixing my sleep fix my blood sugar on its own?

For many people it helps meaningfully, but it works best alongside the nutrition and movement guidance in this course, not as a standalone fix.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice.
