Why This Isn't a Footnote

Two drivers that work independent of your diet

Most diabetes content treats this as a single bullet point. This course gives each factor its own attention.

Stress & Cortisol

Cortisol pushes blood sugar up directly

Elevated cortisol is independently linked to higher blood sugar and worse insulin sensitivity — a stressful week can raise your readings even when your diet hasn't changed at all.

Poor Sleep

Insufficient sleep has the same effect

Insufficient sleep is independently linked to higher blood sugar and worse insulin sensitivity too — which is why a bad night can show up in your numbers the next day, separate from stress.
