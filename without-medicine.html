The honest, structured answer

For some people, sustained lifestyle change can reduce or eliminate the need for medication — this is called remission, and it's real, published, and documented. For others, medication remains part of long-term management no matter what they do, often due to how long they've had diabetes or other factors outside their control. Neither outcome is a personal failure.
