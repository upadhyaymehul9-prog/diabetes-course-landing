Tired all the time, even after a full night's sleep? Your blood sugar might be why.

Fatigue is one of the most common, least-discussed symptoms of Type 2 diabetes. Big swings in blood sugar — not just high readings — leave you drained. Stabilize the swing, and energy usually follows.

Before you ask

Could my tiredness be something other than blood sugar?

Possibly — fatigue has many causes. This course helps you see whether your own pattern tracks with your glucose readings, which is useful information to bring to your doctor either way.

Will this fix my fatigue completely?

Many people find stabilizing blood sugar meaningfully improves energy, but this isn't a guarantee and isn't a substitute for ruling out other causes with your doctor.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice.
