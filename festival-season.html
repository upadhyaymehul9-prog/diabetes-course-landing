A Different Approach

What actually helps during festival season

Pick your moments, not every moment

You don't need to say yes to every sweet at every function — choosing one or two occasions to genuinely enjoy beats restricting everywhere and breaking down somewhere.

Never arrive on an empty stomach

Showing up to a function hungry almost guarantees overeating. A small protein-containing snack beforehand changes the whole evening.

Walk after the meal, not just before

A short walk after a heavy festival meal — even 10 minutes — measurably helps blunt the blood sugar spike.

Keep your medication and sleep timing as normal as possible

Food is often the focus, but irregular sleep and skipped or delayed medication during busy festival days affect your numbers just as much.

The Part Most People Get Wrong

One festival doesn't undo weeks of progress

What people assume

"I ate badly for three days, so my progress is ruined and I have to start completely over."

What actually happens

A few days of festival eating causes a temporary bump, not a reset. Going straight back to your normal structure the next day matters far more than punishing yourself for the festival itself.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Should I just skip festivals entirely to stay safe?

For most people, that's not necessary or sustainable long-term — the course focuses on realistic strategies for participating, not permanent avoidance.

What if I eat more than planned at a function?

Get back to your normal structure at the very next meal — one meal or one day doesn't require "fixing" with restriction, just a return to your regular plan.
