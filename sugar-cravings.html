Before you ask

Will this mean giving up sugar completely?

No. The focus is on meal structure and timing that prevents the spike-and-crash cycle — not permanent, all-or-nothing restriction, which usually backfires anyway.

What if my cravings are at night, not after meals?

Night-time cravings are often linked to how your last meal of the day was structured, plus sleep quality — both of which this course addresses directly.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice.
