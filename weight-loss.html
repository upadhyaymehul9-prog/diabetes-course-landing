Before you ask

How much weight loss is actually needed?

The DiRECT trial found the highest remission rates around 15kg of weight loss, but improvements in blood sugar were seen at lower amounts too — this isn't an all-or-nothing target.

Is this a crash diet?

No. The course is built around a sustainable, structured calorie approach — not a short-term detox or extreme restriction.

What if my medication causes weight gain?

Talk to your doctor about your options — some medications affect weight differently. This course works alongside your medical care, not instead of it.
