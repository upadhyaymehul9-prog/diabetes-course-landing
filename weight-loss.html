Inside the DiRECT Trial

How much weight loss was linked to remission

Highest Remission Rates
~15kg
In the UK's DiRECT trial, participants who lost around 15kg through a structured programme had the highest remission rates — but meaningful improvement in blood sugar began well before reaching that amount.
Not everyone achieves remission, but the pattern of "more structured weight loss, better glucose control" was clear. This course applies that same structured, evidence-based thinking to the role insulin resistance plays in making the scale stick.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

How much weight loss is actually needed?

The DiRECT trial found the highest remission rates around 15kg of weight loss, but improvements in blood sugar were seen at lower amounts too — this isn't an all-or-nothing target.

Is this a crash diet?

No. The course is built around a sustainable, structured calorie approach — not a short-term detox or extreme restriction.

What if my medication causes weight gain?

Talk to your doctor about your options — some medications affect weight differently. This course works alongside your medical care, not instead of it.
