Before you ask

Is prediabetes definitely going to become diabetes?

No, not definitely — and that's exactly the window this course is built for. Structured lifestyle change at this stage has strong evidence behind it.

Do I need medication at the prediabetes stage?

Some doctors do prescribe medication such as metformin at this stage. This course is education alongside whatever your doctor recommends — never a replacement for their advice.

My numbers are only slightly elevated — is this still for me?

Yes. Earlier action tends to be more effective, not less necessary. The earlier you start, the more room you typically have to work with.
