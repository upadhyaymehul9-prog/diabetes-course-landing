Dr. Mehul Upadhyay has spent 15+ years working inside hospital operations and healthcare quality management — including time in the New Zealand healthcare system. Frequently asked questions

Will they need to be tech-savvy to use it?

No. It's video-based, self-paced, and works on a phone, tablet, or computer — they watch and follow along at their own speed.

Does this replace their doctor?

No. It gives them structure and better questions to bring to their doctor — it's never a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

What if they've had diabetes for years already?

The course works for anyone wanting a real structure — newly diagnosed or years in. The tracking system and nutrition protocol apply either way.
