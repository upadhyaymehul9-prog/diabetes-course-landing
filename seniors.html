Not A Plan Built For A 30-Year-Old

What generic advice assumes vs. what this course accounts for

Most diabetes advice assumes

  • You're young, fit, and on one medication
  • Full mobility and no joint pain
  • Smartphone fluency for tracking
  • Remission is always the right goal

This course accounts for

  • Blood pressure, joint pain, and other long-term conditions alongside diabetes
  • Safe, low-impact movement that works with reduced mobility
  • A simple, low-tech tracking system — pen and paper works just as well
  • Individualised goals: remission-focused or stability-focused, set with your doctor

Dr. Mehul Upadhyay has spent 15+ years working inside hospital operations and healthcare quality management — including time in the New Zealand healthcare system, where structured care for older adults managing multiple conditions is a core part of the system. This course brings that same careful, safety-first thinking home.

Important: Diabetes management goals can reasonably differ with age and other health conditions. This course is educational and does not replace the individualised treatment targets your doctor sets for you.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Is remission still a realistic goal at my age?

It depends on your individual situation. The course covers both remission-focused and stability-focused goals — your doctor is best placed to help you decide which applies to you.

I'm on several medications — is this still safe to follow?

The course is educational and designed to work alongside medical care, not replace it. Always review any diet or activity changes with your doctor, especially with multiple medications.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this course?

No. It's video-based and self-paced, and the tracking system is deliberately simple — pen and paper works just as well as an app.
