Before you ask

I travel for work constantly — will this still work?

Yes. The nutrition and movement protocol is built to flex around travel, hotel food and irregular schedules, not just a stable home routine.

What if I miss a week because of a work crunch?

You resume the structure where you left off — you don't restart from week one. This is covered directly in the course.

Can I do this without cutting out client dinners and travel?

Yes. The framework is built around real decisions at real meals, not a rule that only works if you cook every meal at home.
