Where This Course Stands, Honestly

Not a dismissal of medication as an option

GLP-1 medications have become a major part of the weight-loss and diabetes conversation. This course does not teach, discuss, or evaluate these medications as part of its curriculum — it's built entirely around structured nutrition, movement, sleep and stress management, and tracking.

That's not a dismissal of medication as an option. Whether a GLP-1 medication is appropriate for you is a conversation for you and your doctor, based on your specific situation. What this course offers is the lifestyle-first structure — the same kind of approach behind the DiRECT trial, which found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission.

Lifestyle change and medication are not necessarily an either/or choice. Many people use both together. This course gives you the structured lifestyle piece regardless of what else is part of your treatment plan.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Does this course cover GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic or Mounjaro?

No — this course's curriculum doesn't cover these medications. It's built entirely around structured nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, and tracking. If you want to discuss medication options, that's a conversation for your doctor.

Should I do this course instead of considering medication?

That's not a decision this course makes for you. It gives you a structured lifestyle approach that works alongside whatever your doctor recommends, medication included.

Is the lifestyle approach actually backed by real evidence?

Yes — the course is built around the same structured approach used in the UK's DiRECT trial, which found that structured weight loss and lifestyle change put a meaningful number of participants with Type 2 diabetes into remission.
