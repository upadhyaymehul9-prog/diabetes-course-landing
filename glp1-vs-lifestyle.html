Where this course stands, honestly

GLP-1 medications have become a major part of the weight-loss and diabetes conversation. This course does not teach, discuss, or evaluate these medications as part of its curriculum — it's built entirely around structured nutrition (the plate model), movement, sleep and stress management, and tracking.

That's not a dismissal of medication as an option. Whether a GLP-1 medication is appropriate for you is a conversation for you and your doctor, based on your specific situation. What this course offers is the lifestyle-first structure — the same kind of approach behind the DiRECT trial, which found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission.

Worth knowing: Lifestyle change and medication are not necessarily an either/or choice. Many people use both together. This course gives you the structured lifestyle piece regardless of what else is part of your treatment plan — it's not positioned as a replacement for a conversation with your doctor about medication options.
