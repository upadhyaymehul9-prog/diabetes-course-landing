The two numbers, explained

Fasting glucose
Measured first thing in the morning, before eating or drinking anything except water. Below 100 mg/dL is normal. 100–125 is prediabetic range. 126 or above, on more than one occasion, is diabetic range.
Post-meal (postprandial) glucose
Checked roughly two hours after you start eating. Below 140 mg/dL is normal. 140–199 suggests impaired glucose tolerance. 200 or above is diabetic range.

Think of it this way: HbA1c is like your bank's quarterly statement. Fasting and post-meal glucose are like checking your account balance day to day. You need both views to understand the full picture — if you only check fasting and it looks fine, you could be missing dramatic post-meal spikes entirely. If you only check after meals, you'd miss how your body behaves overnight.
