Before you ask

Will one trip undo my progress?

A single trip is unlikely to undo months of structured change, especially if you have a clear plan to resume your routine afterward — which this course covers directly.

Does this cover medication across time zones?

The course gives you a framework for planning this, but always confirm specific medication timing and dosage adjustments with your own doctor before you travel.

What if I can't track my usual way while traveling?

The tracking system is built to be simple enough to use from a phone anywhere, including without your usual scale or home setup.
