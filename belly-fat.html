Before you ask

Can I target belly fat specifically with exercise?

Spot-reduction (losing fat from one area through targeted exercise alone) isn't supported by evidence. What does work is the combination of nutrition, overall fat loss, sleep, and stress management covered in this course — visceral fat tends to respond well to that combination.

My BMI is normal — do I still need this?

If your waist measurement or blood sugar is a concern despite a "normal" BMI, this is exactly the gap this course addresses. Talk to your doctor about your specific risk.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice.
