Belly Fat and Type 2 Diabetes: Waist Size Limits for Indians

You can have a normal weight and still carry risky fat around your middle. Here is what to measure and what to do.

By Dr. Mehul Upadhyay · Last updated 27 November 2026

Short answer: for South Asians, a waist of 90 cm or more in men and 80 cm or more in women counts as abdominal obesity under International Diabetes Federation criteria.1 Asian Indians tend to carry fat inside the abdomen and in organs such as the liver and muscle, which drives diabetes risk.2 Losing 5 to 7% of body weight helps.3

Why belly fat matters more for Indians

A 2009 consensus statement for Asian Indians describes unique features of obesity in this group: more body fat, more abdominal fat, more fat inside the abdomen, and fat deposited in places such as the liver and muscle.2 It names obesity as a major driver of the metabolic syndrome and type 2 diabetes in Asian Indians.2

That is why a person can look slim, have a normal BMI and still be at risk. See also young-onset diabetes and PCOS and insulin resistance.

These are the IDF cut-offs used for abdominal obesity.1

Ask your doctor or nurse to show you how to measure your waist the same way each time. Write it down with your other numbers.

BMI alone can miss the problem

A 2025 revision of the definition of obesity for Asian Indians living in India says the older guidelines relied only on BMI. Its stage 2 obesity criteria require a BMI over 23 kg/m² and at least one of an excess waist circumference or waist-to-height ratio.4 In plain words: BMI and waist size should be read together.

See also weight loss with diabetes for the WHO Asian BMI action points.

The liver connection

Fat deposited in the liver is part of the same picture. Roughly 70% of people with type 2 diabetes have fatty liver, called MASLD, and many have never been told.5 See fatty liver and type 2 diabetes.

What helps

  1. Aim for 5 to 7% weight loss if you have overweight or obesity. The ADA advises it, with more benefit up to about 15%.3 For a 90 kg person, 5% is 4.5 kg.
  2. Move after meals. See the post-meal walk and movement.
  3. Eat more vegetables, legumes and whole grains, and less sugary drinks and refined grains.3 See an Indian diet chart.
  4. Fix sleep and stress. See stress, sleep and blood sugar.
  5. Re-measure your waist every month and watch the trend.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

What waist size is unhealthy for Indians?

Under International Diabetes Federation criteria for South Asians, abdominal obesity is a waist of 90 cm or more in men and 80 cm or more in women.

Can I have belly fat and a normal BMI?

Yes. Asian Indians often carry more fat inside the abdomen and in organs at a lower BMI, so waist size is worth measuring as well.

Does belly fat cause type 2 diabetes?

It is closely linked. Consensus guidance names obesity as a major driver of metabolic syndrome and type 2 diabetes in Asian Indians.

How do I lose belly fat?

Overall weight loss, regular movement, a diet rich in vegetables, legumes and whole grains, and good sleep. The ADA advises at least 5 to 7% weight loss for people with overweight or obesity.

How fast will my waist shrink?

It varies. Track your waist monthly rather than weekly, and look at the trend along with your sugar readings.

Is belly fat linked to fatty liver?

Yes. Fat deposited in the liver is part of the same pattern, and fatty liver is very common in people with type 2 diabetes.
