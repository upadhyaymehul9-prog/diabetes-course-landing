Frequently asked questions

Does the course prevent complications?

No course can promise that. Risk depends on many factors and requires appropriate, ongoing medical care — this course is education alongside that care, not instead of it.

Is this a replacement for regular check-ups?

No. Keep every recommended appointment, screening and test. This course helps you prepare for them, not skip them.

What if I already have a complication?

Discuss it directly with your treating clinician. The course cannot make individual treatment decisions, but it can help you understand the bigger picture around your care.
