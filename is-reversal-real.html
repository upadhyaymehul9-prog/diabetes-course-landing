Why you're right to be skeptical

  • You've seen ads promising to "cure" or "reverse" diabetes permanently in weeks
  • Doctors themselves have publicly warned patients about expensive reversal programs that overpromise
  • You don't want to pay for hope that isn't backed by anything real
  • Most health content online contradicts itself from one article to the next

What the actual evidence says

There is no cure for Type 2 diabetes. That's medically accurate, and no honest course will tell you otherwise. What's real is remission — bringing blood sugar down to non-diabetic range through sustained lifestyle change, without needing it to be permanent or guaranteed for everyone.

The evidence: The UK's DiRECT trial found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission — not everyone, and not as a "cure," but a real, measurable, published result. That's the standard this course is built on.

What this course will never tell you

  • We never use the word "cure" — diabetes management is ongoing, even in remission
  • We don't promise remission for everyone — some people get there, some don't, and both outcomes are covered honestly
  • We don't ask you to stop your medication — that decision is between you and your doctor
  • We don't sell a miracle product, supplement, or secret ingredient
