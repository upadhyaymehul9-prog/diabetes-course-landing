Before you start

Do I need to test constantly?

Testing frequency depends on your clinical situation and treatment. Follow your clinician's instructions.

Will tracking diagnose a problem?

No. Home data can provide useful information, but diagnosis and treatment decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals.

Can I stop medication if readings improve?

No. Medication changes must be made with your prescribing clinician.
