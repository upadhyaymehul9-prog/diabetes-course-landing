Scattered numbers vs. one connected record

Scattered readings

Glucose checks live in one app, weight on a bathroom scale, blood pressure somewhere else — no connection between any of them, and no way to spot a pattern.

One connected system

Glucose, weight, blood pressure, stress, sleep, walks and plate-model adherence logged in one place, so the pattern behind your numbers actually becomes visible.

Before You Start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to test constantly?

No. Testing frequency depends on your clinical situation and treatment — follow your clinician's instructions. This course focuses on making the readings you already take more useful.

Will tracking diagnose a problem on its own?

No. Home data provides useful information, but diagnosis and treatment decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals.

Can I stop medication if my readings improve?

No. Any medication changes must be made together with your prescribing clinician — never based on home tracking alone.
