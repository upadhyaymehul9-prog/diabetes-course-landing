Before you ask

Does this course tell me how much I can drink?

No. It explains how alcohol generally affects blood sugar so you can make informed decisions, and gives you the questions to ask your own doctor about your specific situation and medication.

I'm on medication — is this still relevant?

Yes, but some diabetes medications have specific interactions with alcohol. Always confirm with your doctor or pharmacist what applies to your exact prescription before changing your habits.

Is the plan all about restriction?

No. The goal is informed decisions around real social situations, not a blanket rule to never drink again.
