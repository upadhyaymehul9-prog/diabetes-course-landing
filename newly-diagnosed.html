Before you ask

I was only diagnosed a few days ago — is it too early to start?

No. Earlier is generally better. This course is built to be a starting point from day one, not something you wait months to begin.

Do I need to stop my medication to follow this course?

No, and you should never stop or change medication without talking to your doctor first. This course works alongside whatever your doctor has prescribed.

I feel overwhelmed — will this add to that?

It's built to do the opposite. The whole point is to replace scattered, conflicting information with one clear structure you follow in order.
