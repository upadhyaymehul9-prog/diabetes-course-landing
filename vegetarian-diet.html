Vegetarian Diet

Vegetarian Diet for Type 2 Diabetes: What the Trials Show and an Indian Plan

Many Indians are vegetarian. Here is what the evidence says, and how to build a plate that actually works.

By Dr. Mehul Upadhyay · Last updated 27 November 2026

Short answer: trials suggest vegetarian diets can lower HbA1c a little. One meta-analysis of randomised trials in people with diabetes found a drop of about 0.29 percentage points.1 An older one found about 0.39.2 The effect is modest, the studies are small, and "vegetarian" is not a cure on its own. What you eat in place of meat matters most.

What the trials show

A 2019 meta-analysis pooled nine randomised trials with 664 people who had diabetes. Vegetarian diets lowered HbA1c by 0.29 percentage points and fasting glucose by 0.56 mmol/L. They also lowered LDL cholesterol.1

An earlier 2014 meta-analysis of controlled trials lasting at least 4 weeks found a pooled HbA1c drop of 0.39 percentage points in type 2 diabetes.2 A newer review of 7 randomised trials found benefits for blood sugar and body mass index, and a possible chance of needing less medicine.3

Meta-analysis of controlled trials (2014)2−0.39 points
Meta-analysis of randomised trials (2019)1−0.29 points

For what HbA1c numbers mean, see HbA1c explained.

Honest limits

  • The effect is modest. A drop of 0.3 to 0.4 points is helpful, but it is not a reversal.
  • Many of the studies were short or small. The authors of the 2014 review said this themselves.2
  • The trials compared vegetarian diets with other diets, often with weight loss as part of the change. It is hard to separate the effect of "no meat" from the effect of eating less overall.
  • Most trials were not done in India. Indian vegetarian food is often high in rice, roti and fried snacks.

The weight-loss evidence is stronger. See weight loss and diabetes and is reversal real?

Getting enough protein without meat

The ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians advise a variety of pulses, nuts, milk and eggs or fish for protein and nutrients.4 The American Diabetes Association also puts legumes, nuts and seeds, and lean proteins among the foods to emphasise.5

SourceIdeas (our suggestion)
PulsesDal, rajma, chole, chana, moong sprouts
DairyCurd, buttermilk, paneer (in small portions)
EggsFor those who eat them
Nuts and seedsA small handful a day

See what to eat by food group for the dal evidence, and the Indian diet page for rice, roti and thali building.

Where vegetarian plates go wrong

  • Too much refined carbohydrate: maida, white bread, poha and white rice with a little sabzi.
  • Fried snacks: namkeen, samosa, kachori and farsan.
  • Sweets, sugary chai and juice. See sugar, jaggery or honey?
  • Heavy gravies: paneer in cream or butter adds many calories.
  • Little protein: a plate of roti and a thin dal will not keep you full.

The ADA advises minimising ultra-processed foods, sweets, sugary drinks and refined grains.5

A sample vegetarian day

This is our suggestion, not a tested menu. Portions depend on your weight and medicines.

MealIdea
BreakfastMoong chilla with curd, or vegetable besan cheela
LunchSabzi, salad, a big bowl of dal, 1 to 2 whole-grain rotis
SnackSprouts chaat, or a handful of nuts
DinnerMixed vegetable and chana or rajma, buttermilk, one roti

Safety

  • If you take insulin or a sulfonylurea, a big change in food can cause low sugar. See low blood sugar.
  • Ask your doctor whether you need to check vitamin B12 and other nutrients on a vegetarian diet.
  • Do not stop any medicine on your own.
Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Does a vegetarian diet lower blood sugar?

A meta-analysis of randomised trials in people with diabetes found a modest HbA1c drop of about 0.29 percentage points. The effect is small and the studies were short.

Can a vegetarian diet reverse diabetes?

There is no strong proof of that. Weight loss has the best evidence for remission, and a vegetarian diet can be one way to eat less and eat better.

How do I get protein as a vegetarian with diabetes?

Use dal, rajma, chana, sprouts, curd, buttermilk, small portions of paneer, eggs if you eat them, and a small handful of nuts.

Is paneer good for diabetes?

It is a protein source, but it is also high in calories and fat. Use small portions and avoid creamy gravies.

Why is my vegetarian diet not helping my sugar?

Check for refined carbohydrates, fried snacks, sweets, juices and large rice portions. A vegetarian plate can still be high in sugar.

Do I need supplements on a vegetarian diet?

Ask your doctor. Vitamin B12 is the usual question. Do not start supplements without advice.
