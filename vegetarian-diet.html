Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for strict vegetarians?

No. The framework works whether you eat dairy and not eggs, eat eggs but not meat, or follow a fully plant-based diet — it's built around flexible protein sources, not one fixed list.

Will I need protein supplements or powders?

No special products are required. The course focuses on whole-food protein sources you can get from a regular Indian or vegetarian kitchen.

Does a vegetarian diet make diabetes harder to manage?

Not inherently — but it does require a different strategy than most generic diabetes diet advice provides, which is exactly what this course addresses.
