Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Is this a liver detox program?

No — and detox teas and supplements aren't a substitute for the weight loss and insulin-sensitivity improvements that actually help fatty liver. This course focuses on what the evidence actually supports.

Do I need a special "liver diet" separate from a diabetes diet?

Generally no — the same structured, sustainable weight-loss approach helps both conditions, since they share the same underlying driver.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice, especially for monitoring your liver.
