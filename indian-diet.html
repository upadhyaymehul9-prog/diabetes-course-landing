15+ Years Healthcare Ops International Hospital Experience
The evidence: The UK's DiRECT trial found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes — built on portion control and food structure rather than any single "forbidden" food — put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission. The same principles of portion and pattern apply regardless of which cuisine you're eating.

Before you ask

Do I need to stop eating rice or roti completely?

No. The framework is about portion and sequencing within your existing meals, not elimination of staple foods.

Will this work with festival food and family functions?

Yes — festivals and family meals are covered directly in the course, rather than ignored or treated as automatic failures.

Is this only useful for people living in India?

No. It works for anyone eating an Indian-style diet, wherever they live.
