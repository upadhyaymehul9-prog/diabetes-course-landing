The Real Difference

A list of bans vs. a framework you can actually use

The "banned foods" approach

  • A fixed list of foods to avoid, with no room for your own preferences
  • No explanation of why — just rules to follow and break
  • Ignores that identical meals affect different people differently
  • Usually impossible to maintain for more than a few weeks

This course's framework

  • Portions and patterns you apply to food you already cook and eat
  • Plate-building guidance, not a forbidden-foods list
  • A way to test and track how specific foods affect your own numbers
  • Built to work alongside your actual life, not instead of it

Before You Start

Frequently asked questions

Does the course give me a fixed meal plan?

No. A single rigid plan can't account for everyone's preferences, culture, medications and schedule — instead you get a framework you apply to your own food.

Can I still eat carbohydrates?

The course covers nutrition structure and glucose patterns rather than declaring any one food group universally good or bad.

Will this cure my diabetes?

No course can guarantee a cure or remission. This course gives you a structured, evidence-informed approach — the outcome depends on many individual factors.
