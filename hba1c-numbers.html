Before you ask

Is my HbA1c number an emergency?

This course can't assess your individual situation — if you're worried about a specific result, contact your doctor directly. What the course can do is help you understand ranges and build a structured response once you've spoken with them.

How much can I realistically expect my number to move?

It varies significantly by individual. The course is built around the same approach used in the DiRECT trial, with no guaranteed outcome — results vary from person to person.

My doctor already explained my number — is this course still useful?

Yes. Most appointments explain the number in under a minute; this course goes further into what to actually do about it, week by week.
