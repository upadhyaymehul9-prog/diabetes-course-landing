Three Numbers, Three Different Conversations

The same report, read three different ways

An HbA1c result isn't one fixed meaning — a 6.8 sits in a different conversation than a 9.2. This course breaks down what different ranges mean in plain English, instead of leaving you to guess.

Early Range
6.8

Often the number that starts the conversation about prediabetes or an early diagnosis — the stage with the most room to work with.

Mid Range
7.5

A number that usually means an existing diagnosis needs real structure, not just a one-line comment at a follow-up.

Higher Range
9.2

A higher-risk number — one that calls for a clear, structured plan and a proper conversation with your doctor about next steps.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Is my HbA1c number an emergency?

This course can't assess your individual situation — if you're worried about a specific result, contact your doctor directly. What the course can do is help you understand ranges and build a structured response once you've spoken with them.

How much can I realistically expect my number to move?

It varies significantly by individual. The course is built around the same approach used in the DiRECT trial, with no guaranteed outcome — results vary from person to person.

My doctor already explained my number — is this course still useful?

Yes. Most appointments explain the number in under a minute; this course goes further into what to actually do about it, week by week.
