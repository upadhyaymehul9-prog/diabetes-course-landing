The Evidence

Not just another YouTube video

A well-known UK study — the DiRECT trial — found that structured weight loss and lifestyle changes put a meaningful number of people with Type 2 diabetes into remission. Not everyone. But enough people that it's worth trying seriously, with the right structure.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. This is structure and knowledge to have better conversations with your doctor — never a replacement for medical advice. Always talk to your doctor before changing medication, diet, or exercise routines.

Is remission guaranteed?

No, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something. Remission doesn't happen for everyone. This course gives you the best structured shot at it, based on real clinical evidence.

Do I need any equipment or special diet?

No special products required. The nutrition and movement protocol is designed to work with what you already have access to.
